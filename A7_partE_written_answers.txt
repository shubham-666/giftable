1. Why do we use private attributes?
Private attributes help protect an object's data from being changed directly from outside the class.

2. Why validate marks?
Validation prevents invalid marks, such as 150 or -5, from being stored.

3. Why use CSV and JSON?
CSV is useful for tabular data and spreadsheets. JSON is useful for storing structured data such as lists and dictionaries.

4. What does finally do?
The finally block runs whether an exception occurs or not, so it is useful for cleanup or a final status message.
