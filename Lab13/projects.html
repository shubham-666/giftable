Python Projects

What I built, what I used, and what I learned

1. Mini Library System

What it does: Manages a small collection of books and supports adding, borrowing, and returning books.

What I used: Python classes and objects, lists/dictionaries, file handling, JSON, and CSV.

What was challenging: Keeping each book's availability correct after borrowing or returning it, and saving updated information so it can be loaded later.

What I learned: How to divide a program into classes and methods, validate user actions, and store data in files.

2. Fest Trophy

What it does: Takes team scores, calculates totals, and selects the team with the highest score.

What I used: Python input/output, loops, lists or dictionaries, numeric comparisons, and conditional statements.

What was challenging: Handling ties and checking that score input is valid before comparing teams.

What I learned: How to process repeated input, compare results, and handle edge cases clearly.
