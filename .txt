Which Part B problem did you choose, and why that one?

I chose B1, Largest and Second Largest. I chose it because it was interesting to solve without storing the numbers in a list. It made me think about how to keep track of the largest and second largest values while reading each number.

Describe one thing that did not work the first time, what you thought was wrong, and what was actually wrong.

The first time, I was confused about how to handle the case where the largest number appears twice. I initially thought the second 45 should automatically be the second largest. After reading the requirement carefully, I decided to treat the second largest as the second distinct-largest value, so the answer for 12, 45, 45, 7, 38 is 45 and 38.

In one sentence each: when would you use a while loop, and when would you use a for loop?

I would use a while loop when I need to repeat something until a condition becomes false and I do not know exactly how many repetitions are needed.

I would use a for loop when I know the range or collection of values I need to go through, such as repeating something five times.
